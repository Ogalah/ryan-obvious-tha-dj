The story

About Ryan

From bedroom DJ to media brand.

Origin

The Music Came First.

Ryan Obvious Tha DJ started where every great DJ does — a bedroom, a pair of decks, and an obsession with making people feel something. What began as a teenage passion project quickly evolved into a weekend hustle, and the weekend hustle became a full career fuelled by word-of-mouth, relentless work, and an instinctive ability to read a crowd.

Over time, the gigs got bigger. The stages got larger. The audiences got broader. But something more interesting was happening in the background — a community was forming. People weren't just coming to events; they were following Ryan online, engaging with his content, sharing his mixes, and showing up because of him specifically.

That's when Ryan made the shift from entertainer to media brand — and everything changed.

"I realised I wasn't just a DJ you hired — I was someone people followed. That distinction changes everything about how you position yourself and the value you bring."

— Ryan Obvious Tha DJ
Today

A Media Property.

Ryan Obvious Tha DJ now operates across two distinct but complementary lanes: elite live entertainment for events from private weddings to corporate functions and festivals — and brand partnerships for companies looking to reach a genuine, engaged community of 150,000+ people across platforms.

Based in Johannesburg with a national footprint and growing regional presence, Ryan is positioned at the intersection of entertainment, culture, and commerce.
